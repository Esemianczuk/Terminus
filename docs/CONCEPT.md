# Terminus

One persistent organism with many windows into its life. His attention belongs to him. He is usually resting; sometimes he notices something, grips a part of his graph, shifts colour or leaves a short inscription. The observer can develop hypotheses about his behaviour without being handed a translation.

Every visible behaviour should feel like evidence of an inner life.

- Current direction: cute and cheeky, moderately androgynous leaning male. Smaller, less glossy irises; restrained blue-green lids; no eyelashes or swept eyeliner wings. Preserve warmth at close range and clarity at pet scale.

## Organism, rather than assistant

No tasks, XP, hunger meter, streaks, productivity goals or affection score. No large language model selects a canned persona. Repetition changes familiarity and suppresses novelty; a prediction surprise can alter attention without guaranteeing an immediate performance. Internal cycles and sleep consolidation provide causes that are not always visible. Repeated poking is an event in that ecology, not a demand that must be obeyed.

The vocabulary is a fictional ancient-looking conlang with stable internal semantic families. It is not presented as a recovered historical language. The habitat shows inscriptions without a translation key; the code and optional observatory remain available for inspection.

## What constitutes evidence

The first implementation starts the recurrent predictor from seeded random weights. Incoming event transitions and sleep replay update its weights locally. This is online prediction of a limited event stream, not understanding, consciousness or a trained general model. Predictor tests verify learnability on controlled repeating sequences. Observational intrigue and long-term behavioural character require living with it; neither is established by those tests.

The graph and stochastic behaviour policy are explicit software. Learned prediction error, familiarity, recency and internal state influence that policy. The animation expresses the result. Device clients share the same revision and identity; they do not train independent pets.
